
# Advertiser Persona Management

## Overview
Advertiser Persona Management allows retailers to define and 
enforce spending rules, category restrictions, and targeting 
permissions at the advertiser level from the Control Center.

## Why this matters
Retailers need guardrails to ensure advertisers stay within 
agreed category boundaries and spending tiers. Previously this 
required manual intervention from the support team.

## How to enable
1. Go to Control Center → Advertiser Persona Management
2. Click Create New Persona
3. Define allowed categories, max daily budget, targeting types
4. Assign persona to one or more advertiser accounts
5. Rules are enforced automatically at campaign creation

## Configuration Options
- Category whitelist and blacklist
- Maximum budget caps daily and lifetime
- Allowed ad formats: Sponsored, Display, Offsite
- Geo-targeting restrictions

## Notes
- Changes to a persona apply immediately to all assigned advertisers
- Advertisers see restricted options in their UI automatically
- Support team can override persona rules with admin access
