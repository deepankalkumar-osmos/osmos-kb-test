# New Reporting Dashboard — Pulse Analytics

## Overview
Pulse Analytics is the new unified reporting dashboard that 
consolidates campaign performance, spend, and ROAS metrics 
into a single view across all ad types.

## Why this matters
Previously advertisers had to switch between Sponsored Products 
and Display Ads reports separately. Pulse brings everything into 
one place with cross-campaign comparison.

## How to enable
1. Go to Control Center → Reports → Pulse Analytics
2. Select date range and campaign types to include
3. Use the Module filter to view by ad type
4. Export to CSV or schedule automated email reports

## Key Metrics Available
- Impressions, Clicks, CTR
- Spend, Revenue, ROAS
- New to Brand orders
- Share of Voice by category

## Notes
- Data refreshes every 4 hours
- Historical data available from account creation date
- Custom metric columns can be saved as views
