# Introducing Campaign Duplication

## Overview
Campaign duplication allows advertisers to copy an existing 
campaign with all its settings, targeting, and creatives intact.
Available for Sponsored Product Ads and Display Ads.

## Why this matters
Setting up campaigns from scratch is time consuming for advertisers 
managing multiple brands or seasonal promotions. Duplication reduces 
setup time by 80% for recurring campaigns.

## How to enable
1. Go to Campaign Manager in the Control Center
2. Select the campaign you want to duplicate
3. Click the three-dot menu on the right → select Duplicate
4. A copy is created with suffix "Copy of" in the name
5. Edit the name, budget, and dates → click Save

## Benefits for Advertisers
- Faster campaign setup for recurring promotions
- Consistent targeting and creative settings across campaigns
- Reduced human error on complex campaign configurations

## Notes
- Duplicated campaigns start in Draft status by default
- Budget and schedule must be updated before activation
- Creative assets are copied but can be individually edited
